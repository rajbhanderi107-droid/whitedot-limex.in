## Recipe
1. **Subject** — concrete and specific, no hype. e.g. "LIMEX trial samples for
   <application> — next steps" or "Re: <their topic> — spec + trial window".
2. **Open** — one line stating who White Dot is and why this email (their inquiry /
   the application). No pleasantries padding.
3. **Body** — 2–4 short paragraphs or tight bullets: the relevant LIMEX fact, what a
   trial involves, what you need from them (volume, application, target use case).
4. **One clear ask / CTA** — a single next step (book a call, confirm application for
   a sample, reply with target volume). Offer WhatsApp as the fast lane.
5. **Sign-off** — plain. "White Dot LLP · Authorized LIMEX Marketing & Sales ·
   WhatsApp +91 88497 28938 · whitedotindia.in".

## Guardrails
- One ask per email. Don't stack CTAs.
- No invented numbers, certifications, or claims. Trial-specific impact only.
- No emoji, no exclamation hype, no "just checking in" filler.
- Keep it skimmable: a procurement reader scans in <15 seconds.
