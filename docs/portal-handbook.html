1. The big picture

The portal is one system that runs the whole business. Everything that used to live in WhatsApp chats, Excel sheets and memory now lives in one place:

  • Every lead from the website lands here automatically — inquiry forms, quote requests, sample requests and calculator submissions on whitedotindia.in flow straight into the portal, with the marketing source attached (so we know if a lead came from a Google ad, WhatsApp link, or a referral).
  • Sales runs on a visual pipeline — drag a lead card from "New" to "Won" like moving sticky notes on a board.
  • AI helps, humans decide — the AI can write follow-up emails and WhatsApp messages, but nothing is ever sent until a person approves it in the Approval Center.
  • Everything is recorded — every change is in the Audit Log. Failed logins and suspicious activity show up in CyberShield automatically.
  • It works on phone and desktop — same address, same login.
One sentence for new staff: "If you did something for a customer and it is not in the portal, it did not happen."

2. Logging in & understanding the screen

Logging in

1
Open whitedotindia.in/#/admin in any browser (Chrome/Edge recommended).
2
Enter the email and password the Super Admin created for you, or use "Sign in with Google" if your Google account was added.
3
You land on the Command Center — the dashboard.
Never share your password. Each person has their own account so the system knows who did what. After 5 wrong password attempts the system blocks logins for a while and records it as a security event.

3. The safety system — read this before touching automation

The portal can automate work (follow-up messages, lead scoring, alerts). Automation is powerful, so every automation and every AI agent runs in one of 5 safety modes:

ModeMeaning in plain words
OFFDoes nothing. Completely asleep.
DRAFTPrepares work and saves it as a draft. Sends nothing. Good for testing.
APPROVAL (default)Prepares work and puts it in the Approval Center. A human must press Approve before anything goes out. This is our standard mode.
AUTOActs by itself within strict daily limits. Only for low-risk, well-tested automations. Needs management decision.
LOCKDOWNEverything frozen. Set automatically by the Emergency Stop or by a critical incident.

The red Stop button (Emergency Stop)

Top-right of every screen. Pressing it puts the whole portal into Lockdown: all automations pause, nothing external is sent, and approving items is blocked by the server itself (not just hidden in the screen). Use it if something is sending wrong messages, if you suspect a hack, or if a serious mistake is spreading.

Lockdown is also automatic: if anyone declares a SEV0 or SEV1 incident in Incident Response, the system locks itself down instantly.

The Approval Center — the human checkpoint

Everything the AI writes (follow-up emails, WhatsApp drafts, proposals) lands here as a card showing exactly what would be sent. You read it, then Approve or Reject. Nothing leaves the company without this step. During Lockdown, Approve is blocked; Reject always works.

Golden rule: AI drafts, humans approve. No exceptions.

4. Every module explained (35 tools)

Overview

Command Center — everyone

The home dashboard. Business numbers, security posture and automation health in one executive view. Start your day here.

WhiteDot AI Brain — managers

Control room for all AI activity: how many drafts the AI made, how many are waiting for approval, the approval rate, and the governance rules every agent must follow.

Growth & Sales

Lead Generation — sales, marketing

Shows where leads come from (Google ad, WhatsApp, referral…) as a chart, and includes a UTM link builder: create a tracked link for each campaign so the portal can tell you which campaign actually brought customers.

CRM & Pipeline — sales (main tool)

The sales board. Every lead is a card; columns are stages (New → Contacted → Qualified → … → Won/Lost). Drag a card to change its stage — it saves automatically. The "AI draft" button on a card asks the AI to write a follow-up email, which goes to the Approval Center.

Inquiries — sales

The raw list of all website inquiries with full details, notes and history. Click any row to open the lead's detail page.

Quote Requests — sales

Customers who asked for pricing on the website. Work them like inquiries; change status as you progress.

Sample Requests — sales + operations

Customers who asked for LIMEX material samples. Operations dispatches; status tracks Requested → Approved → Dispatched → Delivered.

CPQ / Quotations — sales

Create formal quotations: line items, GST, discounts, validity date. Track each through Draft → Sent → Accepted/Rejected → Converted to order.

Orders & Invoices — sales + operations

Confirmed orders with payment and dispatch status. The end of the sales journey and the start of the operations journey.

Customers

Companies — sales

The company & contact directory — every business we talk to, with city, status and contact person.

Customer Portal — managers

Admin view of client accounts — which companies are active clients and their self-service settings.

Calculator — sales

Submissions from the website's sustainability calculator. These are warm leads — they already computed how much plastic and CO₂ they could save with LIMEX.

Follow-Ups — sales

Your to-do list of scheduled follow-up calls/messages with due dates. Check it every morning.

Marketing

Marketing Tools — marketing

Content helpers and the marketing toolkit for day-to-day promotional work.

Campaign Center — marketing

Plan and track campaigns: channel, budget, spend, leads generated, dates and status (Draft → Active → Completed).

Ad Intelligence — marketing

For paid ads (Google/Meta/LinkedIn/YouTube): computes cost-per-lead and budget pacing, and highlights overspending campaigns.

Social Studio — marketing

The social content calendar — plan posts, captions and reels; schedule and track status.

Landing Pages — marketing

Campaign landing page content: plan, draft and track pages used for specific campaigns.

SEO Growth — marketing

Press one button to audit the live website: 11 real checks (title, descriptions, images, schema, speed…) with a 0–100 score and exactly what to fix.

Sustainability Engine — sales + marketing

Plastic/paper/CO₂ calculators and client-facing ESG report material with editable assumptions. Use it to build the sustainability story for a client meeting.

Product & Data

Product PIM — operations

The official product catalog — every LIMEX grade with specs, status and pricing info. One source of truth for what we sell.

Inventory & Samples — operations

Stock levels and sample stock. Rows turn red when stock falls below the reorder point.

Documents — everyone

Brochures, datasheets, certificates — the company's document library.

Google Analytics — marketing, managers

Website traffic data from Google in the portal.

Business Intelligence — managers

12-month lead trend chart, the sales funnel (how many leads reach each stage), top industries, and totals for quotes/orders/campaigns. Real numbers from the live database.

Data Warehouse — managers

Browse all recorded activity grouped by type — the analytical view of everything that happened.

Automation

HyperAutomation — managers only

The control room for the 10 predefined automations (lead scoring, follow-up reminders, reactivation…). Each one has its own enable switch and safety mode, with daily limits.

Workflow Builder — managers

Build custom "when X happens → do Y" rules (trigger, condition, action) and save them to the server.

AI Agents — managers only

The marketplace of AI helpers grouped by domain. Switch each agent on/off and set its safety mode.

Approval Center — managers + senior sales

The human checkpoint. Every AI draft and automation output waits here for Approve/Reject. Check it at least twice a day.

Integrations — super admin

Connections to outside services (Google Analytics, Search Console, WhatsApp, e-mail…) and whether each is in sandbox or production.

Security & Reliability

CyberShield — managers

Security center: a coverage score of implemented protections, plus a live threat feed — real failed logins, rate-limit trips, rejected payloads and lockdown blocks, with severity colors.

Incident Response — managers

Declare and manage incidents with severity SEV0 (worst) to SEV4 (minor). SEV0/SEV1 automatically locks the portal down. Walk the incident through stages until Resolved.

Website Health — operations, managers

One-click health check: database speed, website reachability, sitemap/robots files, configured services.

BugShield — everyone

Report website/portal bugs with severity. Anyone who spots a problem files it here so it is never forgotten.

DevSecOps — technical

Live status of code deployments from GitHub — when the site was last updated and whether it succeeded.

Backup & DR — super admin only

Row counts of every data table and a one-click JSON export of all business data for an off-platform copy.

Administration

Team & Roles — super admin only

Create staff accounts, set their role, deactivate leavers.

Audit Logs — admin+

The permanent record: who did what, when. Cannot be edited by normal users.

Notifications — everyone

Your in-portal notification inbox; mark read individually or all at once.

Settings — admin+

Website settings: brand logo, public-site options, content toggles.

5. Daily workflows — step by step

Workflow A — A new lead arrives (Sales)

1
A visitor submits a form on whitedotindia.in → it appears in Inquiries and as a card in CRM & Pipeline ("New" column), with the marketing source attached. A notification appears on the bell.
2
Open the card. Read what they need. Call or WhatsApp them the same day.
3
Drag the card to Contacted. Optionally press AI draft — the AI writes a follow-up email and parks it in the Approval Center.
4
Manager reviews the draft in Approval Center → Approve → send it.
5
As the deal progresses, keep dragging: Qualified → Proposal → Negotiation → Won (or Lost, with a note why).

Workflow B — Quote to Order (Sales)

1
Customer wants pricing → open CPQ / Quotations → New quotation: line items, GST, discount, validity.
2
Send the quotation; set status Sent. Follow up via Follow-Ups with a due date.
3
Customer accepts → status Accepted → create the order in Orders & Invoices.
4
Operations tracks payment and dispatch on the order until Delivered.

Workflow C — Sample dispatch (Operations)

1
Request appears in Sample Requests → review the application and address.
2
Check material in Inventory & Samples. Low stock (red row)? Flag for reorder.
3
Approve → dispatch → update status with courier details → mark Delivered.
4
Tell the salesperson — sample delivered means a follow-up call within 3 days (set it in Follow-Ups).

Workflow D — Running a campaign (Marketing)

1
Create the campaign in Campaign Center (channel, budget, dates).
2
Build a tracked link in Lead Generation → UTM builder and use that link in the ad/post.
3
Plan content in Social Studio / Landing Pages.
4
Watch results: Lead Generation shows which source brings leads; Ad Intelligence shows cost-per-lead; BI shows the month trend. Update spend in Campaign Center weekly.

Workflow E — Something is wrong (Anyone)

1
Small bug (typo, broken button)? File it in BugShield with severity.
2
Serious problem (wrong messages going out, suspected breach)? Tell a manager → press Emergency Stop → declare an incident in Incident Response with the right SEV level.
3
SEV0/SEV1 locks the portal automatically. Work the incident through its stages until Resolved, then clear the lockdown.

Workflow F — Morning routine (Managers)

1
Command Center — scan the three health rings and KPIs.
2
Approval Center — approve/reject pending drafts.
3
CyberShield — glance at the threat feed (anything red?).
4
BI — check the funnel; where are leads getting stuck?

6. Roles — who can do what, and who to assign

Every account has exactly one role. Roles are set in Team & Roles (Super Admin only). The server enforces these rules — they are not just hidden buttons.

SUPER ADMIN — the owner

Assign to: Raj only (and at most one trusted backup person).

Everything, plus exclusively: create/edit/deactivate user accounts · export full data backup · delete audit logs · delete incidents · manage integrations. The only role that can change who has access.

ADMIN — general manager

Assign to: business/operations manager, senior marketing manager.

Everything daily: full CRM, quotations, orders, campaigns, automations, AI agents, approvals, incidents, settings, audit logs, deleting records. Cannot manage user accounts or export backups.

SALES — sales executive

Assign to: every salesperson.

Work leads end-to-end: inquiries, CRM pipeline, quote/sample requests, CPQ, orders, companies, follow-ups, documents, sustainability engine. Can request AI drafts (approval still needed by a manager). Cannot delete records, change automations, or touch settings.

OPERATIONS — dispatch & stock

Assign to: warehouse/dispatch staff, back-office.

Product PIM, inventory, sample dispatch, order fulfillment status, website health, documents. Cannot delete records or manage campaigns/automations.

VIEWER — read only

Assign to: accountant, auditor, advisor, intern in week 1.

Can open and read dashboards and lists. Cannot change anything.
