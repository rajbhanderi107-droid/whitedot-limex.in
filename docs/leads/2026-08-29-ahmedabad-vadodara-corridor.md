# Lead batch — 2026-08-29 — Ahmedabad → Vadodara corridor (NH-48 / NE-1)

**Brief:** find companies and factories making **opaque plastic products**, **blow-moulded milky/white HDPE bottles**, and — as the main subject — **thin-wall containers**, in locations between Ahmedabad and Vadodara.

**Source:** WebSearch + WebFetch over public company sites, GIDC estate directories (Savli, Makarpura), the GSPMA member register, and B2B directories (IndiaMART / TradeIndia / ExportersIndia). No paid data.

**Corridor definition used:** south-east edge of Ahmedabad (Ramol, Vatva, Bakrol, Aslali) → Kheda / Nadiad → Anand & Vitthal Udyognagar → Vasad → Vadodara (Por on NH-8, Makarpura GIDC, Savli–Manjusar, Waghodia).

Field view (route-book style, sortable and searchable): **Thin Wall Corridor** artifact.

## Headline finding

The corridor splits cleanly into two different businesses, and it matters for how we pitch:

- **Thin-wall / IML is an Ahmedabad-end cluster.** All four thin-wall houses found sit inside the first 20 km (Ramol, Kathwada, Bakrol). Nothing equivalent surfaced in Nadiad, Anand or Vadodara.
- **From Kheda onward it is opaque HDPE blow moulding** — bottles, jars and jerry cans for pharma, agrochemical and dairy. Higher tonnage, less decoration, and an easier material-qualification conversation than food-contact thin wall.

So a single drive can cover both, but they are two separate pitches, not one.

## Notes & caveats

- **Kilometres are approximate corridor positions** from the SE edge of Ahmedabad, for ordering a drive — not odometer readings. Savli–Manjusar and Waghodia sit off the highway on the Vadodara ring.
- **"Directory listed" addresses go stale** and a few directories list traders as manufacturers. Call before driving to any Tier 1/2 entry not marked *Site verified*.
- **Dedupe against prior batches:** Star Plast (2026-07-27, email drafted) and Sona Technoplast (2026-07-21, no contact found) already exist in the pipeline. Treat this drive as follow-up for both.
- **No large dedicated thin-wall IML converter was found in Vadodara or Anand** despite repeated searching. If one exists it is not publicly indexed — the mould makers in Tier 3 are the fastest way to find out.
- **Anand is Amul's home district**, which is where dairy-tub and curd-cup volume actually lives. Duraplast already lists dairy bottles and tubs. That is the strongest volume story on the corridor.
- MSME district industrial profile PDFs for Anand and Kheda (dcmsme.gov.in) were attempted as a source; the Anand file did not parse. Worth a manual read if a deeper Kheda/Anand sweep is wanted.
