# Board 01 — Pure Aesthetic Cinematic

Beauty first. Texture, light, skin, stone, dust, surface. No explanation.

- **S1 (0:00–0:04):** The grain of raw limestone in one shaft of light; dust suspended like time. Frost-white highlights on iron-grey-green dark.
- **S2 (0:04–0:08):** Shadowed silhouettes, rim-lit; restraint as elegance — pressure implied, never shown ugly.
- **S3 (0:08–0:13):** Soft vapor and pale particles; the air itself feels mineral. Volumetric, weightless.
- **S4 (0:13–0:18):** Warm skin meets cool stone; a fingertip and a vein — the most human, most tactile frame of the film.
- **S5 (0:18–0:25):** Light gliding across refined surfaces; product as sculpture; cream and frost tones.
- **S6 (0:25–0:31):** A sheet held to window light; translucency, daylight on a real face.
- **S7 (0:31–0:36):** A calm sunlit table; stillness, breath, a single leaf.
- **S8 (0:36–0:40):** Darkness, faint texture, type. Silence as luxury.

**Aesthetic rules:** shallow DOF, soft rim light, real imperfection, subtle grain, generous negative space, no oversaturation.
