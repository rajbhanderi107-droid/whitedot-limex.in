# Director's Treatment — BORN OF LIMEX

## Logline
Every material is a choice. BORN OF LIMEX traces a single mineral from silence to responsible product — a calm, tactile, premium 40 seconds that makes sustainability feel inevitable, not preachy.

## Reference north stars
Apple environmental films (restraint, macro intimacy) · Japanese material-innovation films (TBM/LIMEX corporate calm) · Porsche design documentaries (engineered luxury) · high-end B2B material brand films.

## Tone
Calm, intelligent, responsible, premium. Emotional but never dramatic. Scientific but never cold. The film should feel hand-made and real — limestone, skin, dust, light — not "an AI video".

## Visual thesis
Dark cinematic luxury with soft mineral-white highlights. The mineral is the protagonist; the human hand is the turning point; the product is the payoff; WhiteDot is the bridge to adoption.

## Pacing philosophy
Breath-led. Long, slow opening (silence), a gentle build through origin and birth, a confident mid-section of real products, a warm human/business beat, then a quiet, earned close. Cuts are *motivated by the material* (dust, grain, vein, light) — never template wipes.

## Camera language
ARRI Alexa look, anamorphic feel, 85mm macro, shallow DOF, slow dolly-in, controlled handheld micro-movement, soft rim light, real skin texture, real material imperfection, subtle film grain.

## What success looks like
A film a premium global agency could sell to a material-innovation company: realistic, tactile, emotional, brand-safe, and instantly usable as the website hero + social launch.

## Hard restrictions
No cyberpunk, neon, fantasy magic, hologram overload, cartoon, greenwashing, fake-lab cliches, glowing crystals, pollution-disaster montage, exaggerated VFX, cheap stock feel, plastic-looking limestone, robotic hands, or inaccurate claims.
