# Board 05 — Transition Narrative (7 cuts, Google-Flow ready)

Every transition is *motivated by the material*. Durations @24fps.

1. **S1->S2 Dust dissolve** (12f): floating dust thickens and reforms as the shadowed packaging field. From: macro dust. To: silhouettes. Curve: ease-in-out.
2. **S2->S3 Mineral particle match-cut** (10f): a shadow edge becomes a stream of falling mineral particles. From: silhouette edge. To: vapor/particles. Curve: linear-to-ease.
3. **S3->S4 Surface texture morph** (14f): settling particles morph into the slab's veined surface. From: particle field. To: slab surface. Curve: ease-out.
4. **S4->S5 Hand-touch light spread** (12f): light blooms from the fingertip and wipes into the product table. From: contact point. To: first product. Curve: ease-out bloom.
5. **S5->S6 Macro-to-product match cut** (8f): a product edge aligns to the held sheet in the boardroom. From: product edge. To: sheet in hand. Curve: hard-soft.
6. **S6->S7 Laptop-reflection transition** (12f): the laptop screen reflection blooms into daylight on the future table. From: screen glow. To: sunlit table. Curve: ease-in-out.
7. **S7->S8 Logo reveal through mineral veins** (16f): veins draw the wordmark out of the texture into the dark close. From: product/stone. To: logo lockup. Curve: ease-in settle.

**Breath cuts:** allow 2–4f of near-black between S1 open and S8 close only — premium punctuation, never mid-action.
