# Sound Design & Music

**Philosophy:** sound is part of the story. Low cinematic drone, tactile mineral textures, real human handling, generous silence. No EDM, no trailer boom, no cheap corporate stock music.

## Music
- Bed: a single evolving low drone in a minor-but-hopeful tonality; a soft sustained pad enters at S4 (the hand), warms through S5–S6, resolves at S7, and tails to silence by 0:40.
- Sourcing: Suno ("calm cinematic ambient drone, minimal, premium, sustainable brand film, soft warm pad, no drums, no melody hooks, 40 seconds") or licensed library (Musicbed/Artlist "ambient/cinematic minimal"). Keep -20 to -24 LUFS under VO.

## Per-scene sound
| Scene | Ambience | SFX | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | room tone | one soft mineral grain tick | near-silence, establish drone |
| S2 | soft industrial air | distant low hum | restrained, slightly cold |
| S3 | pressurized air swell | fine granular shimmer | sub-bass breath |
| S4 | warm room | hand-on-stone brush, soft tonal lift | emotional peak of texture |
| S5 | studio quiet | gentle tactile clicks, light "set-down" | rhythm builds subtly |
| S6 | boardroom presence | sheet handling, soft page/lcd | human warmth |
| S7 | open daylight air | single gentle swell | resolve |
| S8 | silence | final low resolve, soft tail | end on quiet |

## Transition SFX
Soft mineral "whoosh" (filtered noise) on dust/particle transitions; a quiet light "bloom" on S4->S5; near-silent breath cut allowed only at open/close.

## Mix targets
Dialogue/VO -14 LUFS integrated; music ducked under VO; true peak <= -1 dBTP. Deliver stems: VO, music, ambience, SFX.
