# AI Video Prompts (image-to-video, per scene)

Use the matching still from `ai-image-prompts.md` as the start frame. Keep motion subtle and premium; durations match the shot list. Global negative: no warping, no morphing faces/hands, no neon, no cartoon, no text artifacts, no jitter.

### S1 (4s) — Silence
Slow breath-like push-in on macro limestone; dust drifts gently through the light shaft; almost imperceptible parallax. Camera: 85mm macro, slow dolly-in. Mood: still, premium.

### S2 (4s) — Pressure
Slow lateral dolly past shadowed packaging silhouettes; soft rim light travels along edges; restrained motion. No fast moves.

### S3 (5s) — Carbon/Mineral
Push-in through soft vapor; fine pale particles fall and settle like slow snow; volumetric light shifts subtly. Gentle, weightless.

### S4 (5s) — Birth of LIMEX
Dolly-in as particles compact to a slab; a real hand enters and touches; rack focus to natural veins blooming under the fingertip. Warm, tactile, no finger distortion.

### S5 (7s) — Transformation
Series of gentle push-ins/match-moves across products on a table; soft light glides over each surface; clean, commercial, real.

### S6 (6s) — Adoption
Warm handheld micro-movement; a sheet lifts to window light (translucency); rack focus to a laptop showing a dark minimal site. Documentary realism.

### S7 (5s) — Cleaner Future
Slow rising push-in toward window light; daylight bloom grows across finished products; serene.

### S8 (4s) — Brand Close
Near-static, almost imperceptible push-in on dark textured plate; hold for logo/tagline comp; settle to black.
