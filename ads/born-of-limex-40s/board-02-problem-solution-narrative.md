# Board 02 — Problem to Solution Narrative

Contrast arc: **dark pressure -> mineral transformation -> refined product.**

- **Problem (S1–S2):** The weight of convenience. Plastic and paper dependence shown as quiet shadow and pressure — premium, never garbage. The viewer feels the cost without being lectured.
- **Turn (S3–S4):** Pressure becomes possibility. Carbon/mineral origin and the human touch reframe the problem as a *choice* we can remake. The hand on stone = agency.
- **Solution (S5–S6):** LIMEX as the calm answer — real sheets, packaging, bottles, FMCG forms — adopted by real people and businesses, not fantasy.
- **Resolution (S7–S8):** A cleaner material future, responsibly stated, signed WhiteDot x LIMEX.

**Message discipline:** reduces plastic/paper dependence; a responsible material pathway. No overclaim.
