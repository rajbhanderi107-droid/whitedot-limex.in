# Board 03 — Overall Story (one continuous film)

We open in darkness. A single shaft of light finds raw limestone, and dust hangs in the air like a held breath — *every material begins with a choice.* For decades the world chose convenience; we glimpse it only as shadow, stacked and silent. Then the air changes: soft vapor, invisible pressure, becomes fine pale mineral falling like snow — carbon pressure turning into mineral possibility.

The particles gather and settle into a refined slab, and a real human hand reaches in and touches it. The surface answers with natural veins. This is the birth of LIMEX — where nature and science meet. Light spreads from that touch across a product table: a smooth sheet, a packaging surface, a clean bottle, an everyday package, a paper-like box — each real, each tactile.

Around a calm table, people lift a sheet to the light, study it, and turn to a laptop showing WhiteDot — innovation meeting adoption. Daylight blooms across finished products beside raw stone and a single leaf: quietly, responsibly, beautifully. We return to darkness, faint mineral texture behind a clean mark. Born of LIMEX.
