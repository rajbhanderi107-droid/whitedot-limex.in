A bobbin for the textile industry, produced by injection moulding from a blend of 40% LIMEX PE78-02M + 60% PP homo-polymer. PE78-02M is a LIMEX compound that is itself ~78% calcium carbonate, so the finished bobbin carries roughly 31% limestone by mass — replacing petroleum plastic while holding the precise tolerances a high-speed spinning bobbin demands.
